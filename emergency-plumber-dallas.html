What to Do During a Plumbing Emergency

Take these steps to limit damage while you wait for our technician to arrive. Safety first — if you smell gas or see sparking, leave the home and call 911.

Shut off the main water valve

Usually located near the water meter, in the garage, or where the line enters the house. Turn clockwise until it stops. This stops water flow to the entire home.

Turn off the water heater

For gas units, set to "pilot." For electric, flip the breaker. This prevents the tank from running dry and burning out heating elements.

Open faucets to drain lines

Open cold-water taps on the lowest floor to relieve pressure and drain remaining water from the pipes.

Contain the water

Use buckets, towels, and a wet/dry vacuum. Move valuables and electronics away from the affected area. Document damage with photos for insurance.
