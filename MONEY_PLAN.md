# SEO Money Plan — "Money Printing v1"

## 3 monetizable niches (pick one)
1. **AI tools for small business** (affiliate + lead gen) — high intent, recurring SaaS commissions, growing search demand.
2. **Home services lead gen** (plumbing/HVAC/roofing) — high CPC, local intent, sell leads at $20–$80/lead.
3. **Finance calculators/tools** (ads + affiliate) — evergreen, high RPM, programmatic SEO potential.

**Recommendation:** Start with #2 (home services lead gen) — fastest path to revenue, local SEO is winnable in 60–90 days, no content treadmill.

## 90-day playbook (home services lead gen)

### Month 1 — Foundation
- Pick 1 city + 1 service (e.g., "emergency plumber [city]"). 
- Build 1-page site: service + city in H1, NAP, click-to-call, form. Target Core Web Vitals <2.5s LCP.
- Technical SEO: HTTPS, mobile-first, schema (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ), sitemap, robots, Google Business Profile.
- On-page template: Title "Emergency Plumber in [City] | 24/7 | [Brand]", meta, H1, 800+ words, FAQ, testimonials, CTA above fold.

### Month 2 — Content + authority
- 12 supporting pages: service-area pages (5 neighborhoods), "cost of X in [city]", "how to choose a plumber", FAQ hub, emergency guide.
- Internal linking: money page ← all supporting pages; breadcrumbs; footer links.
- Citations: Yelp, Angi, BBB, local chamber, industry directories (NAP consistency).
- Reviews: ask 10 past customers for Google reviews.

### Month 3 — Scale + monetize
- Add 2 more cities (cloned template, unique content).
- Start selling leads: call-tracking number (CallRail) → route to 2–3 local contractors at $30–$60/qualified call.
- Track: rankings, calls, form fills, cost-per-lead.

### Link building
- Local sponsorships, chamber of commerce, supplier directories, guest posts on home-improvement blogs, HARO/Connectively.

## Monetization model
- Primary: sell qualified leads/calls to contractors (pay-per-lead).
- Secondary: affiliate for tools/parts, display ads once traffic >5k/mo.
- Timeline: first revenue typically week 6–10; $1k–$3k/mo by month 4 if ranking top 3 locally.

## Quick wins this week
1. Claim & optimize Google Business Profile.
2. Buy exact-match-ish domain (service+city) and host on fast static site.
3. Write the money page + 3 supporting pages.
4. Set up CallRail tracking number.
5. Submit to 10 local directories.

## KPIs
- Rankings for 5 money keywords, organic sessions, calls/forms, CPL, revenue.
